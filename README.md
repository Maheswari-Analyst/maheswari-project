# Indian E-Commerce Pricing & Revenue Growth Analysis

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes pricing, sales performance, and revenue growth of an Indian e-commerce business.  
The analysis focuses on understanding revenue trends across categories, states, sales events, gender, and competition intensity to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue**: ₹2.17 Billion
- **Total Orders**: 31,000
- **Average Discount**: 38.11%

## Dashboard Insights

### 1. Revenue Trend Over Time
- Revenue shows overall growth from 2023 to 2025 with some quarterly fluctuations
- Peak revenue was observed in recent quarters of 2025

### 2. Revenue by Category
- **Premium Lifestyle** and **Electronics** are the top revenue-generating categories
- Electronics also contributes the highest share of units sold (approximately 53%)

### 3. Revenue by State
- **Delhi NCR** leads in total revenue, followed by Karnataka and Gujarat
- Revenue declines progressively across other states

### 4. Impact of Sales Events
- Average revenue per order is significantly higher during **Festival** sales compared to Normal periods
- Both Male and Female customers show similar patterns during Festival events

### 5. Competition Intensity Analysis
- Categories with **Medium** competition intensity generate the highest revenue
- High competition intensity categories generate relatively lower revenue

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- DAX Measures
- Interactive Visuals (Line chart, Bar charts, Pie chart, Area chart, Cards, Slicers)

# Medical Expenses Insights: Smokers vs Non-Smokers

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes medical insurance charges to understand how smoking status, age, gender, number of children, and region impact healthcare costs.  
The goal is to identify key cost drivers and provide clear insights that can support better decision-making in insurance pricing and risk assessment.

## Key Metrics
- **Total Customers**: 1,000
- **Smoker Rate**: 20.5%
- **Total Charges**: $17.76 Million

## Dashboard Insights

### 1. Smoking Status Distribution
- **79.52%** of customers are Non-Smokers
- **20.48%** of customers are Smokers

### 2. Average Charges by Smoking Status & Gender
- Smokers have significantly higher average medical charges compared to Non-Smokers
- Male smokers show the highest average charges

### 3. Average Charge by Age Category and Gender
- Medical charges increase with age for both males and females
- Males generally have higher average charges than females across most age groups

### 4. Impact of Children on Charges
- Average charges vary based on the number of children and smoking status
- Smokers with children tend to have higher medical expenses

### 5. Regional Analysis
- **Southeast** region has the highest average charges
- **Southwest** region has the lowest average charges

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power Query)
- DAX Measures
- Interactive Visualizations (Bar charts, Pie chart, Donut chart, Area chart, Cards, Slicers)
