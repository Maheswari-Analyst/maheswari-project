# Indian E-Commerce Pricing & Revenue Growth Analysis

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes pricing, sales performance, and revenue growth of an Indian e-commerce business.  
The analysis focuses on understanding revenue trends across categories, states, sales events, gender, and competition intensity to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue**: ₹2.17 Billion
- **Total Orders**: 31,000
- **Average Discount**: 38.11%

## Dashboard Insights

### 1. Revenue Trend Over Time
- Revenue shows overall growth from 2023 to 2025 with some quarterly fluctuations
- Peak revenue was observed in recent quarters of 2025

### 2. Revenue by Category
- **Premium Lifestyle** and **Electronics** are the top revenue-generating categories
- Electronics also contributes the highest share of units sold (approximately 53%)

### 3. Revenue by State
- **Delhi NCR** leads in total revenue, followed by Karnataka and Gujarat
- Revenue declines progressively across other states

### 4. Impact of Sales Events
- Average revenue per order is significantly higher during **Festival** sales compared to Normal periods
- Both Male and Female customers show similar patterns during Festival events

### 5. Competition Intensity Analysis
- Categories with **Medium** competition intensity generate the highest revenue
- High competition intensity categories generate relatively lower revenue

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- DAX Measures
- Interactive Visuals (Line chart, Bar charts, Pie chart, Area chart, Cards, Slicers)
